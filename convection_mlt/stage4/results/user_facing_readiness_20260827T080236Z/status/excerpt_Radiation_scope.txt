Radiation scope (isothermal cell-centred source)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Radiation uses a piecewise-isothermal, cell-centred source
representation. This is a consistent finite-volume discretization
whose adequacy is established through optical-depth-adapted grids
and demonstrated spatial convergence. It is not equivalent at finite
resolution to HELIOS non-isothermal within-layer source reconstruction.
The HELIOS N=96 iso=yes vs iso=no+adj counterfactual (different CZ
topology, ~145 K deep-T shift) warns that the radiative-convective
transition is sensitive to within-layer source treatment on a coarse
geometric grid; that result does not transfer directly to the nested-τ
internal track.
